# सारथि — The Storyboard & 7-Scene Production Bible

The single source of truth for the Sarathi story site. The site is a **scroll-story**: a
cinematic gate, then seven beats the visitor descends through. Each beat is anchored by one
**generated scene-video**. You generate the 7 videos (Dreamina / image-to-video, charioteer
character kept consistent); I build the scroll engine + motion design that receives them.

> **Director's lens (the room we're writing for):** Rajamouli's divinity & scale ·
> Nolan's practical-IMAX grandeur & time · Villeneuve's monumental stillness & negative space ·
> Fincher's precise camera · Spielberg's awe on a human face · Miyazaki's warmth.

## The spine (why this story)
In the *Gita*, the warrior Arjuna freezes; his charioteer **Krishna** doesn't fight for him —
he steadies him, reads the field, and names the one true move. **You are the warrior. Sarathi
is the charioteer.** *You drive. He guides.* The journey down the page **travels the body**:
each part of the charioteer is a feature. Awe → Trust → Ownership → Transformation.

## The 7 scenes
Each scene below has: **the beat**, **on-screen**, **director treatment**, **camera**,
**duration**, **how the page uses it**, and a **ready-to-paste video prompt** (+ first/last
frame guidance for image-to-video). Keep the charioteer **identical** across all 7 (use the
same reference image / seed). Palette everywhere: **obsidian black + molten gold (#f5b572) +
teal vault-glow (#2ec4b6/#4cc9f0)**. Render **16:9** (desktop) and a **9:16** crop for mobile.

### Scene 1 — THE CHARIOTEER · "You are the chariot."
- **On-screen:** the charioteer stands in a black void; the gold mandala halo ignites behind
  his head; embers rise. Calm, monumental, eyes to camera.
- **Director:** Villeneuve stillness + Rajamouli halo-ignition. Negative space, one light.
- **Camera:** slow push-in from wide to medium; locked, reverent.
- **Duration:** 5s · loops. **Page use:** the Cinematic Gate hero background.
- **Prompt:**
  > Cinematic slow push-in on a divine obsidian-and-gold charioteer warrior standing in an
  > endless black void, calm and powerful, eyes to camera. A radiant gold mandala halo ignites
  > behind his head, casting warm light. Fine gold embers drift upward, volumetric god-rays from
  > above, shallow depth of field, anamorphic flares. Epic, sacred, mythic scale; ultra-detailed
  > PBR armor, deep blacks, molten-gold highlights. Reverent, awe-inspiring. No camera shake.

### Scene 2 — THE MIND · HERMES · "A coach that remembers."
- **On-screen:** camera rises to the head/halo; the halo ring fills with faint flowing glyphs
  (memory). His gaze tracks, aware. A soft constellation of past "sessions" orbits the halo.
- **Director:** Nolan's intelligence-made-visible; Fincher precision on the eyes.
- **Camera:** low orbit rising to a head-and-shoulders hero angle.
- **Duration:** 5s. **Page use:** Hermes beat (coach-with-memory).
- **Prompt:**
  > Slow rising orbit to a head-and-shoulders hero shot of the same obsidian-gold charioteer.
  > His gold mandala halo fills with faint flowing golden glyphs and a soft constellation of
  > orbiting light-motes, like living memory. Aware, intelligent gaze tracking forward. Warm gold
  > rim light, dark background, volumetric haze, shallow depth of field. Sacred, intelligent, calm.

### Scene 3 — THE HANDS · FORM COACH · "It moves with you."
- **On-screen:** the charioteer raises a hand / shifts into a guiding-motion; faint cyan pose
  lines and joint nodes trace over his arm in real time (on-device coaching motif).
- **Director:** Rajamouli kinetic grace, slow-mo; the tech-overlay reads as divine, not clinical.
- **Camera:** tracking arc around the moving arm/torso.
- **Duration:** 5s. **Page use:** Form Coach beat.
- **Prompt:**
  > The same obsidian-gold charioteer moves with controlled grace — a guiding gesture, hand
  > extended. Faint glowing cyan skeletal pose-lines and joint nodes trace over his arm and torso
  > in real time, like a divine motion-capture aura. Slow-motion, tracking arc around the body,
  > gold and teal light, dark void, embers. Powerful, fluid, sacred-technological.

### Scene 4 — THE HEART · READINESS · "It knows when to rest." ★ (the armor→heart reveal)
- **On-screen:** push to the chest; the armor plates ignite along gold seams and **unlock in
  concentric rings (a blooming mandala) to reveal a living molten-gold heart** beating with a
  teal vault-core at its center. Heartbeat-synced light pulses.
- **Director:** Rajamouli sacred reveal; the emotional climax. This is the armor→heart film.
- **Camera:** slow dolly-in to the chest, settle on the heart.
- **Duration:** 6–8s. **Page use:** the Readiness beat — the centerpiece of the whole site.
- **First frame:** sealed-armor charioteer, teal core dot on chest. **Last frame:** chest open in
  rings, molten-gold heart exposed and blazing.
- **Prompt:**
  > Cinematic slow dolly-in to the chest of the obsidian-gold charioteer. The chest plates ignite
  > along their gold filigree, then unlock and rotate outward in concentric layered rings like a
  > blooming mandala, revealing a radiant molten-gold anatomical heart beating with living light,
  > a glowing teal vault-core at its center. Golden embers, volumetric god-rays, teal-to-gold
  > energy pulses rippling outward with every heartbeat. Sacred, mythic, awe-inspiring. Settle on
  > the glowing heart. Ultra-detailed, deep blacks, molten highlights, shallow depth of field.

### Scene 5 — THE PATH · THE VAULT · "Its memory is yours."
- **On-screen:** the teal heart-core opens into a stream of light that resolves into lines of
  glowing text/script (the Health Vault, open Markdown) flowing outward toward the viewer — the
  charioteer **hands his memory to you**.
- **Director:** Villeneuve scale + Spielberg's gift-giving warmth.
- **Camera:** pull back slightly as the light-script streams toward camera.
- **Duration:** 5s. **Page use:** the Vault beat (ownership, no lock-in).
- **Prompt:**
  > From the glowing teal vault-core in the charioteer's chest, a stream of soft cyan light flows
  > outward and resolves into elegant lines of glowing script and data, like a sacred living
  > ledger offered toward the viewer. The charioteer gestures, calm and giving. Teal and gold
  > light, dark void, drifting particles, volumetric haze, shallow depth of field. Generous, sacred.

### Scene 6 — BECOME · THE TWIN · "Watch yourself level up."
- **On-screen:** the figure straightens and **grows** — musculature/armor strengthens, gold
  filigree brightens, scale increases. Strength made visible, beat by beat.
- **Director:** Rajamouli ascension/power-up; Snyder slow-mo heroism (restrained).
- **Camera:** slow rise (low angle) as the figure powers up.
- **Duration:** 5s. **Page use:** the Twin beat.
- **Prompt:**
  > Low-angle hero shot of the obsidian-gold charioteer powering up: his musculature and armor
  > strengthen and grow, gold filigree brightens and ignites, a surge of golden energy rises
  > through his body. Slow-motion ascension, embers and light streaming upward, dark void, god-rays.
  > Triumphant, monumental, divine. Restrained and powerful, not flashy.

### Scene 7 — THE INVITATION · CTA · "Meet your Twin."
- **On-screen:** the charioteer turns to the viewer and **extends a hand** (the "he guides"
  gesture); the halo flares to full; warm light fills the frame.
- **Director:** Spielberg's open-hand invitation; the emotional landing.
- **Camera:** gentle move to a welcoming medium shot; hand toward camera.
- **Duration:** 5s · loops. **Page use:** final CTA — "Meet your Twin" / sign up.
- **Prompt:**
  > The obsidian-gold charioteer turns toward the viewer and extends an open hand in invitation,
  > his gold mandala halo flaring to full radiance, warm golden light filling the frame. Calm,
  > welcoming, divine. Slow gentle camera move to a medium shot, hand reaching toward camera.
  > Embers, god-rays, shallow depth of field. Hopeful, sacred, inviting.

## Hand-off → what I build (the scroll engine)
1. `SarathiStory.jsx` — a pinned, scroll-scrubbed sequence: the Cinematic Gate, then 7 beats.
2. Each beat: full-bleed `<video>` (scene N) + narrative copy revealing in sync + per-beat accent
   (gold → teal at the heart/vault) + scroll-driven transitions (crossfade, scale, parallax).
3. Reduced-motion / slow connection → poster stills (the charioteer renders) so it never breaks.
4. The body-travel: each beat's camera/copy maps to a body part (head→Hermes, hands→Form,
   heart→Readiness, core→Vault, whole→Twin), so scrolling literally *reads the charioteer*.
