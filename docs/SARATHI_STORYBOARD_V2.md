## Why v1 failed (honest post-mortem)

| Problem | Cause | v2 fix |
|---|---|---|
| **The look felt cheap** | Element powers were drawn in code — dots, glows, lines. Code particles have a hard quality ceiling; they read as "website effects", never cinema. | Every scene becomes a **pre-rendered Kling film clip** derived from the locked photoreal master. Particles demoted to faint ambient garnish only. |
| **The motion felt stiff** | Camera zooms were CSS transforms; scene changes were card swaps; text re-animated on every boundary. | **Scroll scrubs the film** (NRG's actual mechanic): the video's timeline is bound to the scrollbar, so the user's hand drives the transformation frame by frame. Smooth momentum scroll (Lenis-style easing). Camera moves live *inside* the footage. |
| **The story didn't land** | Too many words. Two dense card acts per chapter — a wall of text over a stage. | **≤ 8 words on screen at any moment.** One huge caption line at a time. Myth told in third person ("He forgot…"), product proof in second person ("Sarathi remembers yours."). |
| **Features weren't understood** | The myth never touched the product. | New third beat per chapter: **THE PROOF** — the mythic light condenses into the *real product screen* (actual Vault graph, Form Coach camera HUD, meal log, readiness ring, Twin). Myth → product, every chapter. |

**What stays (it was right):** the charioteer master, the five elements, the lack → gift arc, the phase tracker, the gathered-chips ritual, the shloka anchors.

## The grammar of every element chapter — 3 beats

1. **THE LACK** (first third of the clip) — the warrior without the element. Dim, hollow, failing. One line: the struggle in words anyone recognizes.
2. **THE GIFT** (rest of the clip) — the element visibly enters his body at its centre. Light returns. One line.
3. **THE PROOF** (after the clip) — the light crystallizes into the real Sarathi screen. One line + the actual feature, named.

The **first frame** of each clip = the lack. The **last frame** = the gift received. Kling's first+last-frame mode makes the transformation the video itself — the user scrolls him from powerless to empowered with their own hand.

## SCENE 1 · आकाश — SPACE · The mind that forgot *(Vault + lifelong memory)*
- **First frame (the lack):** the warrior in a cold grey void, head bowed, eyes closed, armor dull. Tiny shards of pale light drift *away* from his head into darkness — memories leaving him.
- **Motion:** the drifting shards halt mid-air. The void above tears open into a deep violet nebula. The shards reverse — a spiral river of starlight streams *back into his crown*. Constellation lines connect above him like a map of everything he ever did. His eyes open, lit.
- **Last frame (the gift):** head raised, eyes open, a luminous constellation crown above him, violet river still feeding it.
- **Captions:** ① "He forgot every battle he had won." ② "So the sky began remembering for him."
- **PROOF:** constellation condenses into the **real Health Vault graph**. "Sarathi remembers yours — every workout, meal and heartbeat, forever."
- Shloka retained: *mattaḥ smṛtir jñānam* · Gita 15.15

## SCENE 2 · वायु — AIR · The breath with no witness *(Form Coach)*
- **First frame:** mid-squat in dull light, posture collapsing — knees caving, back rounding, face strained. Alone. No one watching.
- **Motion:** silver-cyan ribbons of wind circle him like a living coach. They trace along his spine and knees; his posture straightens *as the wind aligns his joints*. He rises through one perfect rep, cloak snapping.
- **Last frame:** upright, chest open, ribbons held around his arms like drawn bowstrings, faint cyan light tracing his perfect alignment.
- **Captions:** ① "No one watched his form — and it failed him." ② "Then the wind itself became his coach."
- **PROOF:** the **real Form Coach camera HUD** (skeleton overlay, rep counter). "Every rep seen, counted, corrected — by your phone."
- Shloka: *prāṇāpāna-samāyuktaḥ* · Gita 15.14

## SCENE 3 · अग्नि — FIRE · The fuel burned blind *(one-line meal logging)*
- **First frame:** he sits by a dead brazier — grey ash, no flame — holding an empty bowl. Drained.
- **Motion:** golden offering-motes (grain, fruit-light) fall into the brazier. Flame ignites and leaps — *controlled, sacred, a yajña*. Its glow travels into his chest and down his arms as fine golden veins. He stands renewed.
- **Last frame:** standing beside the blazing brazier, gold vein-light under the skin of his forearms, calm and powerful. *(Fire lives in the brazier + chest glow — NOT molten belly; anatomical, no lava.)*
- **Captions:** ① "He fed his fire blind — and it burned him." ② "Named rightly, the same fire forged him."
- **PROOF:** the **real one-line meal log** ("2 rotis, dal, curd" → macros appear). "Say what you ate. Sarathi reads the fuel."
- Shloka: *ahaṁ vaiśvānaro bhūtvā* · Gita 15.14

## SCENE 4 · आपस् — WATER · The rest that restored nothing *(readiness)*
- **First frame:** kneeling in shallow black water under a starless sky, exhausted; fine grey fatigue-cracks across his armor; head down.
- **Motion:** the dark ocean *churns* around him (Samudra Manthana). A column of luminous teal water rises and pours into his **heart**. The cracks seal with teal light. He lifts his head. The sea calms to glass; a thin dawn appears.
- **Last frame:** standing on mirror-calm water, heart-centre glowing teal, restored, dawn on the horizon.
- **Captions:** ① "He trained on nights that gave nothing back." ② "Until the ocean returned what sleep had earned."
- **PROOF:** the **real readiness ring** (score + GREEN/YELLOW/RED). "Every dawn: one honest number for how ready you are."
- Shloka: *āpo hi ṣṭhā mayobhuvaḥ* · Rig Veda 10.9

## SCENE 5 · पृथ्वी — EARTH · The work that left no trace *(the Twin)*
- **First frame:** he faces a dark standing stone polished to a mirror. His reflection is *faint, hollow, incomplete* — half-transparent.
- **Motion:** golden dust rises from the earth and flows onto the **reflection**, building it layer by layer — stance, shoulders, presence — until the mirrored figure stands whole and strong. He raises his palm to the stone; the reflection meets it; light passes between them.
- **Last frame:** the two figures palm-to-palm through the mirror — the man and his Twin, both lit gold.
- **Captions:** ① "Nothing showed for his work — so he stopped." ② "The earth built him a mirror that cannot lie."
- **PROOF:** the **real 3D Twin screen** (muscle morphs, level). "Your Twin grows the day you do."
- Shloka: *mātā bhūmiḥ putro 'haṁ pṛthivyāḥ* · Atharva Veda 12.1

## SCENE 6 · THE FIVE IN BALANCE — finale *(CTA)*
- **First frame:** the warrior centered, full figure, five faint colored lights orbiting slowly.
- **Motion:** the five lights strike his five centres in rhythm — crown, lungs, chest-fire, heart, feet — each a soft shockwave. The full aura ignites; the camera pulls back; he stands immense beneath the completed golden mandala, cloak billowing.
- **Last frame:** full figure blazing, five centres lit, mandala halo complete.
- **Caption:** "Five powers. One body. Yours." → tracker morphs to **Begin your Becoming**.

## Motion system (built only after storyboard approval)

- **Scroll-scrubbed video**: each clip's `currentTime` is bound to chapter scroll progress (videos re-encoded keyframe-dense, `ffmpeg -g 12`, for smooth seeking). The user's hand *is* the film's tempo — NRG's core mechanic.
- **Momentum scroll** (Lenis-style eased virtual scroll) so scrubbing feels liquid, never notchy.
- **Captions**: one line, huge display type, ≤8 words, gentle rise+fade at fixed scroll marks. No card walls.
- **Proof cards**: real product UI in a floating frame, slides in during the last 15% of each chapter with the feature pins.
- **Kept**: phase tracker → CTA morph, gathered chips (moved to a slim bottom strip), element rail, shloka as a small side annotation.
- **Fallback**: current particle film remains for reduced-motion and while videos stream in.
