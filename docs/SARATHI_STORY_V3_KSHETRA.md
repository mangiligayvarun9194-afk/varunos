## The frame (the discovery that makes this real)
The Gita's 13th chapter names the body **kṣetra — the field** — and the one who knows it,
**kṣetra-jña — the knower of the field.** And in **Gita 7.4** Krishna catalogs his own nature:
*bhūmir āpo 'nalo vāyuḥ khaṁ mano buddhir eva ca* — **earth, water, fire, air, space — then
mind and intellect.** That is our exact product architecture, spoken 2,300 years ago:
five elements + a guiding intelligence.

> **Your body is the field. Sarathi is the knower of the field.**
> Not a tracker. A charioteer who reads all five elements in you and brings them into balance.

## The story (prose telling — the voice of the site)
Before anything had a name, there was space. Space breathed, and there was air. Air quickened,
and there was fire. Fire condensed into water, and water settled into earth. The Upanishads say
you were built the same way — five elements, layered from the subtle to the solid, wearing a
human face.

Every morning you walk onto a field. The field is your own body. Some days you win it; some
days it wins you. The old warriors had one advantage you don't: a charioteer — one who knew
the field better than the enemy did, who never fought in their place, and never once left.

Sarathi is that charioteer, returned for you. He does not count your steps. He reads your
elements — the space of your mind, the winds of your breath, the fire of your fuel, the tides
of your recovery, the earth of your strength — and he tells you the one true move. You drive.
He guides. And when the five come into balance, you meet the person the field was always
building: your Twin — *the self that is the friend of the self.*

## On-page copy v3 (implemented)
- **Gate subtext:** "Your body is the field where every battle is fought — and won. Sarathi,
  the charioteer, knows the field: he reads the five elements in you and brings them into balance."
- **AKASHA — "Space remembers."** Space is the first-born element — the silence that holds every
  sound. Hermes is your Akasha: every session, every meal, every heartbeat written into living
  memory, so his guidance speaks with your whole history behind it.
- **VAYU — "Air moves you."** From space came air — the five winds that move everything within
  you, breath and body alike. Sarathi reads motion the way a charioteer reads the wind: every
  rep counted, graded and corrected as it happens.
- **AGNI — "Fire transforms you."** The Gita names the fire in your belly: Vaiśvānara, digester
  of all food. Log any meal in one line — Sarathi reads the fuel, balances the burn, and keeps
  the flame clean.
- **APAS — "Water restores you."** The gods churned the ocean and it yielded amṛta — the nectar
  of immortality. Your nights are that churning: sleep, HRV and strain flow into one readiness
  score. Rest when the tide turns; rise with nectar.
- **PRITHVI — "Earth is what you build."** When the healing herb could not be found, Hanuman
  lifted the mountain itself. That is training: bone and muscle built grain by grain, until you
  can carry what once carried you. Your Twin grows visibly stronger as you do.
- **BALANCE — "When the five align, you rise."** Lift yourself by your own self, says the Gita —
  the self alone is the friend of the self. That friend is your Twin. Sixty seconds to begin.

## Craft rules (why this lands)
1. **Quote, never cosplay:** real verses, cited, in transliteration — reverent, not costume.
2. **One myth per beat, told in one breath** (Vaiśvānara, the Churning, the Mountain) — each a
   complete story in ≤2 sentences, each resolving INTO the feature, never beside it.
3. **The product claim always lands last** — myth opens the door, the feature walks through.
4. **No privacy claims** (v2 stance holds): the record is power; Akasha frames data as wisdom.
5. **The Twin = Gita 6.5.** This is the deepest hook in the product: the "self as friend" is not
   our metaphor — it's the Gita's. Investor line: "Our avatar feature is a 2,300-year-old verse."
