## The problem
People now have endless health *data* (watches, rings, scales) but almost no **guidance that
remembers them**. Coaching apps are generic and forget you between sessions; trackers show
charts but never say *what to do next*. The result: low retention, abandoned wearables, and a
market full of dashboards no one acts on.

## The product (built and live, by one founder)
Sarathi is a private AI health OS built on a Bhagavad-Gita metaphor — **you are the chariot,
Sarathi is the charioteer.** Four roles, all shipped:
- **Hermes** — an AI coach *with memory*: it recalls every session, score and PR and speaks the
  single next move. (The wedge. Most apps can't do this.)
- **The Twin** — a living avatar that grows per-muscle as you train; the cinematic homepage tour.
- **Form Coach** — on-device pose AI: counts reps + grades form for [14] lifts, with per-rep
  DTW scoring and a 3D "coach-replay" — runs in the browser, video never leaves the phone.
- **Readiness & the Vault** — one readiness score from sleep/HRV/strain, and a Health Vault in
  open Markdown the user **owns and exports** (no lock-in).
Auth/multi-user, LLM cost guardrails, and legal (Privacy/ToS) are in place.

## Why now / why us
- **Coach-with-memory** is newly possible (cheap LLMs + on-device pose models) and is the
  retention unlock generic apps lack.
- **Privacy as a feature** (on-device coaching, exportable vault) is a real wedge as scrutiny rises.
- **Distribution via Health Connect / Google Fit ingest** reaches far more users than a native
  iOS-only tracker — the basis of an **India-first wedge** (huge, underserved, price-sensitive).
