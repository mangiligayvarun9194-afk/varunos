Safety

VarunOS is a personal-use educational tool. It is not a medical device. Risk tiers are screening estimates, not diagnoses.

If you have symptoms — chest pain, sudden weakness on one side, slurred speech, severe headache, sudden vision loss — call your local emergency number (911 in the US, 108 in India, 112 in the EU) immediately. The app will not do it for you.

If a tier comes back elevated, the action label will be “discuss with your doctor” — not a diagnosis.

Health surveillance (optional)

Surveillance is OFF by default. With it OFF, the app can still compute a risk tier from your BP / glucose / CGM values, but the raw numbers are not stored on the server.

Turn it ON if you want trends, weekly reports, and the doctor-share export to include your raw values.
